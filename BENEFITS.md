# GoreeCloud Identity — Benefits

GoreeCloud Identity is intended to give GoreeCloud one self-hosted, privacy-first authority for principal identity and authentication state while keeping protected credentials under GoreeCloud Vault authority.

Current Development value is limited to a bounded service foundation and an explicit fail-closed account-registration policy. Broader benefits such as single sign-on, revocable sessions, passkeys, device identity, federation, and centralized account lifecycle remain planned until implemented and accepted.
